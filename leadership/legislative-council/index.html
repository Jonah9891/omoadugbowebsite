Ward Representation

The Legislative Council, your voice in government.

Five elected councillors represent the five wards of Badagry West LCDA, debating local bye-laws, approving budgets, and providing legislative oversight.

The Legislative Council is the local government development area's law-making and oversight arm. Councillors, elected from each of the five electoral wards, represent their communities, review public expenditure, and consider local resolutions that touch on markets, sanitation, public infrastructure, and civic life.

Inaugurated alongside the Executive leadership, the 5th Legislative Council operates through principal officers who preside over deliberations and represent grassroots constituency priorities.
