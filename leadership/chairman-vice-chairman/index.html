Executive Profile

Hon. Rauf Ibrahim Kayode Yemaren.

Hon. Rauf Ibrahim Kayode Yemaren is a Nigerian entrepreneur, politician, public administrator and community development advocate. He is the Executive Chairman of Badagry West Local Council Development Area, Lagos State.

His journey through business, grassroots politics and local government administration reflects a longstanding commitment to public service, community advancement and the socioeconomic development of Badagry West.

Early Life and Education

Hon. Yemaren hails from Akoko Community in Badagry West LCDA. He began his education at Methodist Primary School, Enuko, Badagry, completing his primary education in 1981. He then attended Unity Grammar School, Ojo, and proceeded to Lagos State University (LASU), Ojo, where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Biochemistry in 1998. His education, business experience and involvement in community affairs have shaped his approach to leadership and public service.

Business Career & Community Service

Before moving fully into public service, he established himself as an entrepreneur and business executive with interests across different sectors. He also served as Chairman of the Dealers Association, Badagry West LCDA, gaining experience in business leadership, stakeholder engagement and commercial coordination.

Beyond business, he has maintained an active interest in humanitarian service, youth mentorship, educational support and community welfare throughout Badagry West.

Public Service Record

Special Adviser on Revenue and Marketing (2012–2014)

Contributed to revenue-generation strategies, fiscal responsibility and efforts to strengthen the council's revenue base and public service delivery.

Vice Chairman, Badagry West LCDA (2017–2025)

Served two consecutive terms alongside the former administration. Over eight years he participated actively in policy implementation, project supervision, community engagement and grassroots stakeholder relations.

Executive Chairman, Badagry West LCDA (2025–Present)

Inaugurated as Executive Chairman to lead the administration, steering priorities in healthcare, education, rural road grading, sanitation, and social intervention.

Works, Housing & Infrastructure Portfolio

Hon. Sesi Ogungbe Yusuf.

Hon. Sesi Ogungbe Yusuf is the Vice Chairman of Badagry West LCDA. A public administrator and community development advocate, she brings more than 14 years of Lagos State Civil Service experience to the office. She oversees the Works, Housing and Infrastructure portfolio.

Education and Professional Career

She holds a Bachelor's degree and a Master's degree in Human Resources and Industrial Relations from Lagos State University (LASU). Over more than 14 years in the Lagos State Civil Service she served across local government administrations including Amuwo-Odofin and Iba LCDA, gaining deep experience in public administration, community relations and grassroots governance.

Public Service & Mandate

Before her swearing-in as Vice Chairman in November 2025, she served as Supervisor for Women Affairs and Poverty Alleviation (WAPA) in Badagry West LCDA, contributing to programmes for women's economic empowerment, poverty alleviation and support for vulnerable residents. In April 2026 she was assigned oversight of the Works, Housing and Infrastructure Department.

As Vice Chairman, she supports the Executive Chairman in policy implementation, administrative coordination, community engagement and delivery of the council's development objectives.

Guiding Values

Inclusiveness, accountability, compassion, community participation and sustainable development. She is committed to creating opportunities for women, supporting vulnerable groups, investing in education and strengthening the relationship between government and the people.
