Privacy Policy.

A plain and transparent explanation of how we handle information on this website.

What we collect

When you use our contact or complaint forms we collect the details you enter: your name, email, phone number, ward, subject and message.

How we use it

We use it only to read and respond to your message and to improve council services.

Where it goes

Messages are delivered through a form service to the council's administrative email. We do not display your message or details on this website.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies or tracking. Your browser stores only your light or dark display choice.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We may share it with the relevant council office or government agency where needed to respond.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the information you sent by contacting support@omoadugbo.com.

Updates

We may update this notice and will show the date of the latest version. Latest update: 29 July 2026.
