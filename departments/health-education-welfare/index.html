Community Care

Health & Social Welfare.

Ensuring accessible, quality healthcare and protecting the well-being of vulnerable residents represent core commitments of our administration. The council delivers community-level medical support, maternal care facilities, routine immunisation campaigns, and direct welfare assistance.

Key Health & Welfare Initiatives:

  • • Asheri Primary Health Centre: Modern primary healthcare centre under construction in Asheri to provide 24-hour clinical and maternal healthcare.
  • • Medical equipment upgrades: Supplying modern delivery beds, diagnostic tools, and clinical equipment to primary health centres across the LCDA.
  • • Immunisation & epidemic prevention: Routine infant immunisation drives, polio eradication campaigns, and cholera advocacy partnerships.
  • • Social welfare & WAPA programmes: Targeted monthly financial support for persons with disabilities, food palliatives, and assistance for widows led by the Women Affairs and Poverty Alleviation office.

Youth Advancement

Education & Youth Development.

Investing in our youth and strengthening school infrastructure are fundamental pillars of the council's development agenda. Through direct education subsidies, digital training centres, and school safety renovations, the council supports students from primary to tertiary examination levels.

Key Educational Programmes:

  • • Ganyingbo Topa CBT & Tutorial Centre: Constructing a modern computer-based examination hub providing digital literacy training and free coaching for JAMB, WAEC, and GCE candidates.
  • • Academic exam sponsorship: Distribution of free GCE and JAMB examination forms to qualified public secondary school students across all 5 wards.
  • • School perimeter security: Construction of protective boundary fencing at Methodist Primary School, Sawa, and L.A. Primary School, Apa.
  • • Civic & youth engagement: Leadership workshops, sporting initiatives, and civic engagement led by the Supervisor for Youth and Civic Engagement.
