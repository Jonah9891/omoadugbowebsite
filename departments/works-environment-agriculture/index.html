Environmental Stewardship

Environment & Sanitation.

Protecting our coastal ecology, ensuring clean market centers, and maintaining flood drainage are fundamental to public health in Badagry West. The council administration has reintroduced monthly environmental sanitation exercises across all five electoral wards.

Environmental Operations:

  • • Monthly environmental sanitation: Mobilizing communities, transport operators, and market traders to clear drains and keep surroundings sanitary.
  • • Corridor clearing & drainage desilting: Focused environmental works around the Gbaji International Link Bridge and major transportation arteries.
  • • Waste management advocacy: Collaborating with Lagos State waste management authorities to ensure proper disposal and prevent drainage blockages.

Physical Delivery

Works & Infrastructure.

Overseen directly by the Vice Chairman, the Works, Housing and Infrastructure Department drives the delivery of physical projects across all five wards, emphasizing road connectivity, rural electrification, and potable water access.

Infrastructure Mandates:

  • • Inner road grading: Grading and sand-filling of more than 50 community roads across the five wards to ensure year-round motorability.
  • • Gedu Road Phase I: Road construction and reinforced drainage works to mitigate flooding in key commercial axes.
  • • Solar-powered potable water: Industrial borehole projects such as the solar-powered installation in Akoko Community.
  • • Street lighting & power: Distribution and maintenance of solar-powered streetlights along major community corridors.

Rural Livelihoods

Agriculture & Rural Development.

Farming, fishing, and rural agribusiness represent primary economic livelihoods for thousands of households across Badagry West. The council encourages sustainable agriculture and food security as central tenets of our ten-point development agenda.

Rural Agricultural Priorities:

  • • Smallholder farmer engagement: Coordinating with local agricultural cooperatives and farmers' associations to support food security.
  • • Rural access roads: Maintaining inner farm-to-market graded roads to facilitate the swift transport of harvests to commercial market hubs.
  • • Social support for rural households: Providing targeted social interventions, welfare packages, and tools for agrarian communities.
