Civic Administration

Public Services.

Badagry West LCDA provides local administration, lawful revenue administration, technology services, and active inter-agency coordination with Lagos State authorities. Residents and community organizations can contact the central council secretariat for guidance and formal administrative assistance.

What this office does:

  • • Revenue administration and enforcement: Lawful assessment and collection within the council's constitutionally devolved remit under the guidance of the Supervisor for Revenue Matters and Enforcement.
  • • Digital communication and ICT access: Modernizing council public records and information technology under the Supervisor for ICT.
  • • Official press & media relations: Accurate and verified public announcements steered by the Chief Press Secretary.
Have questions regarding municipal services? Contact our administrative desk for procedural guidance.

Local Enterprise

Business & Trade.

Local market traders, transport operators, artisans and small businesses form the backbone of Badagry West's commercial vitality. Given the area's strategic location along the international trade gateway to the Republic of Benin, the council actively supports lawful commerce, marketplace safety, and orderly transport infrastructure.

Council Enterprise Support:

  • • Market and park operations: Regular engagement with market associations and transport unions under the Supervisor for Market and Park Matters.
  • • Empowerment & tools for artisans: Direct council programmes providing sewing machines, deep freezers, work equipment, and small business grants.
  • • Cross-border commerce facilitation: Maintaining sanitary and accessible roads linking local trading hubs with the Seme international corridor.
