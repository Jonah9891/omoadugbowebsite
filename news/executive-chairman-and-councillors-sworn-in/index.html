Executive Chairman and Councillors Sworn In

Executive Chairman alongside five councillors took the oath of office before the Governor of Lagos State.

The newly elected Executive leadership of Badagry West LCDA was inaugurated to steer the grassroots governance and socioeconomic development of the council area.
