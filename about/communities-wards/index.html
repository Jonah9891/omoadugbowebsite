Grassroots Life

The Communities of Badagry West.

Badagry West LCDA is home to communities whose people contribute directly to the area's rich cultural identity, vibrant social life, and dynamic cross-border economic activity. Each community has its own distinct local priorities, and responsive governance begins with recognizing these unique strengths.

The council collaborates actively with community leaders, residents, youth networks, women's cooperatives, market traders, and artisanal associations to encourage inclusive grassroots development and strengthen public trust.

Prominent communities across our council territory include Akoko, Asheri, Ganyingbo Topa, Gbaji, Apa, Kweme, Sawa, Owode, and Seme, forming a vibrant corridor from the lagoon inland to the Atlantic coastline.

Electoral Geography

The Five Administrative Wards.

Badagry West LCDA is organized into five electoral wards, each represented by an elected councillor in the Legislative Council.

Legislative Council →
Ward A

Kweme

Covers Kweme and surrounding coastal settlements along the Atlantic corridor, active in fishing, farming, and coastal trade.

Legislative Seat: Majority Leader
Ward B

Wesere

Encompasses Wesere and neighboring rural farming and trading communities with strong community associations.

Legislative Seat: Deputy Leader
Ward C

Apa

Historic kingdom centre and educational hub, including the Apa community, primary educational institutions, and civic halls.

Legislative Seat: Deputy Chief Whip
Ward D

Akoro

Home to Akoko and Akoro communities, benefiting from key grassroots solar water projects and agricultural enterprise.

Legislative Seat: Council Leader
Ward E

Gbethrome

Strategic ward bordering the Seme commercial corridor, hosting active international markets, trade hubs, and transport links.

Legislative Seat: Chief Whip

International Gateway

Seme Border

One of West Africa's busiest international land borders, carrying trade, travellers and cultural exchange every day across Nigeria and Benin.

Coastal Landmark

Suntan Beach

A popular stretch of coastline along the Lagos–Seme corridor, recognized for tall coconut palms, beach cabanas and Atlantic ocean vistas.

Commercial Axis

Owode-Apa Corridor

A vital arterial corridor linking council communities, trade markets, and regional transport routes.
