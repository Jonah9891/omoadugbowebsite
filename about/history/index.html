Our Story

History and background.

A region shaped by heritage, international trade and enduring grassroots community strength.

Badagry West LCDA is part of the wider Badagry region of Lagos State, an area with a rich history shaped by indigenous communities, longstanding traditions, commerce and cultural interaction across the West African coast.

The wider Badagry region is home to communities with longstanding cultural traditions and connections to the Ogu and Yoruba peoples. Its position near the international border with the Republic of Benin has added to its commercial and social significance over generations.

Over time, local administration in Lagos State has evolved to bring governance closer to communities. Local Council Development Areas are part of this grassroots structure. Badagry West is one of the 37 LCDAs created by Lagos State to extend public service delivery and focus development directly within local wards.

Today, Badagry West LCDA serves its communities through accountable local administration and community-level infrastructure development. Its continuing growth is linked to the needs of residents, the preservation of local heritage, better public facilities, and wider social and economic opportunity.

Evolution

Milestones in local administration.

Foundation Era (2003)

Creation of Badagry West LCDA

The LCDA was created as part of the 37 Local Council Development Areas established by the Lagos State Government to decentralize governance and bring administrative focus closer to coastal border communities.

27 July 2025

Inauguration of Executive Leadership

The Executive Chairman alongside five elected ward councillors took the oath of office before the Governor of Lagos State to steer the local council administration.

November 2025

Vice Chairman Sworn In

The Vice Chairman was sworn into office following legislative screening, overseeing the Works, Housing and Infrastructure portfolio to accelerate community project delivery.

December 2025

100-Day Empowerment Programme

The council launched a major grassroots intervention drive, equipping local artisans with professional work tools, providing financial grants to widows and the elderly, and distributing medical supplies to primary health centres.

11 March 2026

Gbaji–Seme Electrification Commissioning

Lagos State commissioned vital electrification restoration works along the Gbaji–Seme and Owode–Apa corridor, supported by the council administration to energize regional trade communities.

July 2026

First Year of Delivery Milestones

The administration completed comprehensive delivery reviews across rural health, CBT education, and road sand-filling, setting priority fast-tracking for the proposed Gbaji International Market.
