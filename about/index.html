About Us

Badagry West LCDA, serving our communities.

Local government closer to the people, in a region of heritage, coastline and cross-border connection.

Badagry West Local Council Development Area (LCDA) is a local administrative area in Lagos State, Nigeria, serving communities within the Badagry region. As part of the state's grassroots governance structure, the council brings public administration closer to residents and supports community-level development.

Badagry West is situated in the western part of Lagos State, in a region recognised for its cultural heritage, coastal environment, cross-border connections and diverse economic activities.

The council's role covers local administration, community engagement, environmental management, infrastructure development and the coordination of public services within its area, in line with applicable laws and government policies.

Through collaboration with residents, community organisations, businesses and government agencies, the council supports initiatives that improve public facilities, expand economic opportunities and promote sustainable development.

Strategic Purpose

Vision & Mission.

The guiding principles driving our policies, investments and administrative service across Badagry West.

Our Vision

To build a prosperous, inclusive and sustainable Badagry West where every community has opportunities to thrive through responsive governance, quality public services, economic development and improved living standards.

Our Mission

To deliver transparent, accountable and people-centred local administration by improving community infrastructure, supporting social welfare, promoting economic opportunities, protecting the environment and encouraging active participation in governance.

Institutional Integrity

Our Core Values.

Value 01

Conducting public affairs responsibly, honestly and ethically at every level of council administration.

Value 02

Value 03

Value 06

Strategic Agenda

01
Quality Education

Improving educational facilities, supporting students and expanding access to academic assistance.

02
Primary Healthcare

Strengthening healthcare infrastructure and access to essential medical services close to home.

03

Promoting skills development, economic opportunities and practical self-reliance.

04
Economic Development

Supporting traders, artisans, small businesses and local cross-border commercial activity.

06

Promoting sanitation, cleanliness and responsible waste and flood management.

07

Strengthening collaboration with security agencies and grassroots community stakeholders.

08

Supporting vulnerable residents, the elderly and widows through social interventions.

09

Encouraging farming, local food security and productive rural enterprise.

10
Human Capital Development

Investing in youth capacity building, vocational training and educational opportunities.
