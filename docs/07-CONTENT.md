H1: Badagry West LCDA, *serving our communities.*
Lead: Local government closer to the people, in a region of heritage, coastline and cross-border connection.

[Confirmed — council-supplied text]
Badagry West Local Council Development Area (LCDA) is a local administrative area in Lagos State, Nigeria, serving communities within the Badagry region. As part of the state's grassroots governance structure, the council brings public administration closer to residents and supports community-level development.

Badagry West is situated in the western part of Lagos State, in a region recognised for its cultural heritage, coastal environment, cross-border connections and diverse economic activities.

The council's role covers local administration, community engagement, environmental management, infrastructure development and the coordination of public services within its area, in line with applicable laws and government policies.

Through collaboration with residents, traditional institutions, community organisations, businesses and government agencies, the council supports initiatives that improve public facilities, expand economic opportunities and promote sustainable development.

## 3. History & Background — `/about/history/`

Eyebrow: Our Story
H1: History and *background.*
Lead: A region shaped by heritage, trade and community.

[Researched / council-supplied background — council to approve]
Badagry West LCDA is part of the wider Badagry region of Lagos State, an area with a rich history shaped by indigenous communities, traditional institutions, commerce and cultural interaction across the West African coast.

The wider Badagry region is home to communities with longstanding cultural traditions and connections to the Ogu and Yoruba peoples. Its position near the international border with the Republic of Benin has added to its commercial and social significance.

Over time, local administration in Lagos State has evolved to bring governance closer to communities. Local Council Development Areas are part of this grassroots structure. Badagry West is one of the 37 LCDAs created by Lagos State, in the early 2000s. [PLACEHOLDER: official establishment date and wording from council records]
