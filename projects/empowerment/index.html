Delivered Initiative December 2025

100 Days of Renewed Hope, Action and Accountability

To mark the first 100 days of the administration, the council ran an empowerment drive that supported artisans with work tools, gave cash support to traders, widows, the elderly and persons with disabilities, and equipped five primary health centres with medical equipment.

Designed as an equitable, direct intervention across all five wards, this landmark initiative focused on removing barriers for self-employed residents and offering immediate relief to vulnerable community members.

Official Advisory

Stay Informed

Opportunities are announced through our official channels only. Be wary of anyone asking for payment to join a council programme.

The Badagry West LCDA administration never charges fees, registration commissions, or levies for public welfare or student empowerment programmes.
