From the Executive Chairman

A government you can see, feel and hold to account.

Dear residents and friends of Badagry West,

Welcome to the official website of Badagry West LCDA.

Since taking office in 2025, our administration has worked to bring government closer to the people. We are building a modern Primary Healthcare Centre in Asheri and a Computer-Based Test Centre at Ganyingbo Topa. We have extended a helping hand to our widows, our elderly, our traders and our artisans. We have brought back our monthly sanitation exercises and strengthened our partnership with security agencies.

Together, we will turn our promise into progress you can see and feel. This website is our commitment to openness. Follow our projects, meet your leaders and tell us your concerns.

Hon. Rauf Ibrahim Kayode Yemaren Executive Chairman, Badagry West LCDA

Our Story

A council rooted in community and heritage.

Badagry West LCDA is one of the 37 Local Council Development Areas created in Lagos State to bring government closer to the people.

It serves communities across a part of Badagry known for its cultural heritage, its palm-lined coastline and its strategic role connecting Nigeria with the wider West African region.
